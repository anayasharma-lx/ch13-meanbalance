Mean as Balance Point
The mean is not just a calculation — it is the point where the data perfectly balances. Click on the number line to place values and see why the mean is the only balance point.

The red line is the mean. The coloured segments show distances from each dot to the mean. Distances on the left and right always sum to the same value.
